About Us

Established in the year 1998, we “Sakthe Animal Feeds” are involved in manufacturing of S Diamond and S Silver cattle feed pellets. Offered products are prepared using only high-quality ingredients at a well-equipped and sophisticated infrastructure unit. These products are admired for their high purity, accurate preparation, high effectiveness, and long shelf life.

We maintain modern infrastructure at our premises and upgrade instruments in the production unit to meet client needs. Products are checked before final dispatch to ensure purity and quality.

The rich expertise of our leader and mentor, Mr. Natarajan Karupannan, has helped us build a leading reputation. His strong business acumen inspires us to set high standards of performance in providing these products.
